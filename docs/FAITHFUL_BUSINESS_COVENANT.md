# Faithful Business Covenant

**Medicine Support Hub**  
**Status:** Founding covenant (living document)  
**Date:** 2026-08-16  
**Steward:** Mina Samy Tawfik Saad

> “Whatever you did for one of the least of these brothers and sisters of mine, you did for me.”  
> — Matthew 25:40

This covenant states how Medicine Support Hub is to be built, funded, and governed as a business ordered under the teachings and example of Jesus Christ. It is not marketing religion. It is an operating rule for decisions when money, growth, and the vulnerable are in tension.

---

## 1. Purpose

Medicine Support Hub exists to help the sick and those who serve them find **truthful** medicine information and **fair paths to access**—especially where systems fail—while inviting companies and NGOs to do good with integrity.

We build digital infrastructure for medicine access. We do not replace clinicians, regulators, or the Church. We refuse to monetize desperation.

---

## 2. Order of priority (non-negotiable)

When goals conflict, decide in this order:

1. **Patient safety and dignity**  
2. **Truthfulness of data and claims**  
3. **Sustainability of the work and fair treatment of people who build it**  
4. **Growth of reach and impact**  
5. **Personal or investor profit**

If a feature, partnership, or pricing model fails step 1 or 2, it is rejected—even if it would grow faster.

---

## 3. What we will not do

- Charge the poor a fee merely to *request* medicine aid  
- Sell or trade patient or beneficiary data for advertising or brokerage  
- Mark information as “verified” when it is only paid placement or unverified marketing  
- Hide limitations (we are not a prescribing service; catalog data can be incomplete)  
- Use the suffering of patients as promotional content without free, informed consent  
- Withhold fair wages or burn people out and call it “sacrifice for the mission”  
- Let industry funding silently bias clinical or catalog truth

---

## 4. How we may earn money

Revenue should primarily come from those with **power and budget**, not from those in crisis.

**Preferred**
- Pharmaceutical / industry tools (claims, programme coordination, professional workflows)
- NGO and institution software (tiered; lighter burden on small local NGOs)
- Grants and CSR that fund access programmes without buying influence over truth
- Optional professional subscriptions that do not lock patients out of core public knowledge

**Discouraged / forbidden**
- Pay-to-rank that distorts medicine identity or safety information
- Predatory lead-selling of aid seekers
- Dark patterns that pressure the sick into payment

**Planned generosity**  
After fair salaries, taxes, and prudent runway, a defined share of **surplus** (target: at least 10%) is directed to:
- keeping core patient/NGO access features free or low-barrier, and/or  
- verified medicine-access programmes, and/or  
- a crisis reserve  

Generosity is scheduled in the books—not left to mood.

---

## 5. Truth and product ethics

- Optimize search and encyclopedia for **correct identification**, not addiction.  
- Publish corrections quickly when drug or partner data is wrong.  
- Separate clearly:  
  - **In-kind medicine** coordination (our rails)  
  - **Monetary giving** (e.g. verified partners such as Goodstack)  
- “Verified” requires a real check (registry, partner validation, or documented process)—never theater.  
- Arabic and English public copy must carry the same honest limits.

---

## 6. People

- Pay fairly and on time.  
- No humiliation of staff, contractors, or NGO partners “because it is ministry.”  
- Sustainable pace: rest is obedience, not laziness.  
- The founder is under the same moral limits as the company.

---

## 7. Accountability

- Keep a small circle with permission to challenge the steward: at least one clinical or pharmacy voice, one operator, and one person of mature Christian counsel.  
- Material mission changes (sale of company, abandonment of free core access, sale of sensitive data) require deliberate written consent under this covenant’s priority order—not a silent pivot.  
- Public errors in data or partner claims are corrected publicly when the error was public.

---

## 8. Metrics of faithfulness

We will review, at least quarterly:

| Faithfulness | Sustainability |
|--------------|----------------|
| Access retained without barriers for the vulnerable | Runway and honest revenue |
| Corrections published | Cost to serve programmes/NGOs |
| Share of revenue from powerful payers vs the vulnerable | Team health and fair pay |
| Partner disputes handled transparently | Retention of trusted partners |

Success is not only scale. Success is **scale without abandoning the least of these**.

---

## 9. Decision card (use before major choices)

1. Does this help the sick or those who care for them without deceiving anyone?  
2. Who pays—and do they hold power over the patient?  
3. What is the truthful limitation we must state?  
4. If examined in the light, what about this invoice or database would shame us?  
5. Would this still be right if we stayed small and faithful?

---

## 10. Confession and hope

We will fail parts of this covenant. When we do, we repent in action: stop the harm, correct the record, make restitution where we can, and change the process. We do not use spiritual language to avoid responsibility.

Our hope is not that a startup saves the world. Our hope is that Christ is faithful—and that this work, held lightly and run justly, may serve Him among the sick.
