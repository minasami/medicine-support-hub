# Founder letter — A faithful enterprise for medicine access

**For:** Public manifesto / about context  
**From:** Mina Samy Tawfik Saad  
**Platform:** https://medicinesupport.app  
**Date:** 2026-08-16

---

I am building Medicine Support Hub as a business, and I intend to build it under the teachings of Jesus Christ.

That does not mean the product is a church, or that only Christians are welcome, or that we replace doctors, pharmacists, or regulators. It means the **rules of the business**—who we charge, what we refuse to sell, how we handle truth, and who we protect when growth is tempting—are ordered by His way: truthfulness, service to the vulnerable, refusal to exploit the weak, and accountability.

## What the platform is for

Too many medicine-access efforts still run on scattered spreadsheets, closed chat threads, and unequal information. Patients and caregivers need clear information. NGOs need tools. Pharmaceutical companies that want to do good need trustworthy channels—not theater.

Medicine Support Hub aims to be infrastructure:

- truthful medicine information where we can maintain it,  
- pathways for programmes and donations that respect dignity,  
- room for industry to participate without buying the truth.

## How we will try to earn money

We will prefer revenue from institutions and companies that can pay for software and coordination—not fees that block a poor patient from asking for help. Core knowledge and access pathways for the vulnerable should remain as open as we can sustainably keep them.

If surplus remains after fair pay, taxes, and prudent reserves, a portion is committed to keeping access open and supporting verified programmes. Generosity should be planned, not performed for applause.

## What we will not become

We will not sell patient desperation.  
We will not label marketing as verification.  
We will not hide our limits.  
We will not treat staff or partners as fuel for a slogan.

Detailed commitments live in our [Faithful Business Covenant](./FAITHFUL_BUSINESS_COVENANT.md).

## An invitation

If you are a clinician, pharmacist, NGO, or company willing to work with integrity: you are welcome. You do not need to share my faith to collaborate on honest infrastructure.

If you hold me to this letter when I drift, you do me a service.

The sick are not a market segment first. They are neighbors. This company exists to serve that fact with competence and truth—as far as God gives us strength.
