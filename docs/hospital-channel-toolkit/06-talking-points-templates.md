# Talking Points & Positioning Templates

These are **templates**, not scripts. Always adapt to the specific product, account, and relationship stage.

## 1. Opening / Relationship Framing
> “My goal is to understand how we can best support reliable access for your patients and make the pharmacy and clinical teams’ work as smooth as possible.”

## 2. Clinical Positioning (General)
> “The product is positioned for [specific patient segment / line of therapy]. The key differentiators versus current options are [1–2 clear points]. We have supporting data on [efficacy / safety / convenience] that I’d like to review with the clinical team if helpful.”

## 3. Quality & Manufacturing Credibility
> “Quality and consistency are foundational for us. The product is manufactured under [relevant standards]. We can provide the documentation your quality or pharmacy team typically requires.”

## 4. Supply Reliability
> “We understand that stock continuity is critical for hospital formularies. Our supply and distribution setup is designed to minimize disruption, and we have a clear escalation path if any issue arises.”

## 5. Economic / Budget Framing (Soft)
> “Beyond the unit price, we look at the overall cost of therapy and impact on the hospital budget. Happy to walk through a simple budget impact view tailored to your volume assumptions.”

## 6. Formulary / Protocol Discussion
> “We would like to explore inclusion in the formulary / relevant protocols so that physicians who want to use it can do so smoothly. What is the typical process and timeline here, and who are the key stakeholders we should engage?”

## 7. Handling Price / Competitor Objections
> “Price is always an important factor. We aim to be competitive while protecting quality and supply reliability. Can we look together at the total picture — clinical outcomes, reliability, and cost — rather than unit price alone?”

## 8. Localization / National Alignment (when relevant)
> “As a company with strong local manufacturing presence / commitment, we are aligned with the national direction on medicine security and self-sufficiency. This also supports more predictable long-term supply.”

## 9. After Listing – Adoption Support
> “Listing is only the first step. We are ready to support training for pharmacy and nursing teams, provide medical information, and stay close to any real-world feedback so usage can grow appropriately.”

## 10. Closing a Meeting
> “What would be the most useful next step from your side — additional clinical data, a discussion with the central procurement team, or a follow-up with the specialty department?”

---

### Specialty / Complex Product Add-ons (examples)
- Hormones / endocrine: emphasize consistency of formulation, patient monitoring support, and protocol fit.
- Respiratory: device training, technique support, and adherence considerations.
- Injectables / specialty: administration practicality, storage, and nursing workload.

### Account Planning Reminder
Before major meetings, prepare:
- Current formulary status and competitors
- Key clinical and economic stakeholders
- Recent tender or group-level decisions
- Any open issues (stock, complaints, training needs)
- Clear ask for the meeting
