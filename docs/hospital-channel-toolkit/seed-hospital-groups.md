## Groups

### Cleopatra Hospitals Group (CHG)
- **Type:** Private hospital group
- **Geography:** Greater Cairo and Suez corridor
- **Public facilities (examples):** Cleopatra Hospital (Heliopolis), Cairo Specialized Hospital, Nile Badrawi Hospital, Al Shorouk Hospital, El Katib Hospital, Cleopatra October, Cleopatra El Tagamoa, Bedaya IVF, East/West Cairo polyclinics
- **Why it matters for KAMs:** Largest listed private hospital group by beds/operating hospitals; central group procurement and multi-site coverage.
- **Public sources:** cleopatrahospitals.com, EGX disclosures, investor presentations
- **Trust:** seed-public

### Alameda Healthcare
- **Type:** Private hospital group
- **Geography:** Cairo and Giza
- **Public facilities (examples):** Dar Al Fouad (6th of October and Nasr City), As-Salam International Hospital
- **Why it matters for KAMs:** Strategic private accounts with group-level decision making.
- **Public sources:** company and hospital public websites
- **Trust:** seed-public

### Saudi German Hospitals – Egypt
- **Type:** Private hospital group (regional brand)
- **Geography:** Cairo and other Egyptian sites as publicly listed by the operator
- **Why it matters for KAMs:** International-brand private hospital channel with protocol-oriented purchasing.
- **Trust:** seed-public

### Andalusia / Andaluseya Group
- **Type:** Private hospital group
- **Geography:** Cairo and Alexandria (confirm current sites before visits)
- **Why it matters for KAMs:** Multi-city private group; site vs group decisions may differ.
- **Trust:** seed-public

### Magdi Yacoub Heart Foundation / Global Heart Centres
- **Type:** Specialist / foundation hospitals
- **Geography:** Aswan and Greater Cairo projects as publicly announced
- **Why it matters for KAMs:** Specialist cardiac protocols and high clinical standards.
- **Trust:** seed-public

### University hospitals (category)
- **Type:** Public / academic
- **Geography:** National network (115+ university hospitals as publicly cited in higher-education announcements)
- **Why it matters for KAMs:** Protocol-driven, teaching hospitals; automation and UHI linkage increasing.
- **Decision pattern:** Often committee- and protocol-led rather than private-group commercial procurement.
- **Trust:** seed-public-category

### MoHP / public hospitals (category)
- **Type:** Public
- **Why it matters for KAMs:** UPA-driven procurement is the main volume lever; individual hospital listing still matters for adoption.
