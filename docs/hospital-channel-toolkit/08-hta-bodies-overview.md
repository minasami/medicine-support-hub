# Health Technology Assessment Bodies – Field Overview

A practical reference for KAMs, Sales, and Marketing. Use this when stakeholders mention international HTA or compare local decisions with overseas systems.

## Global / Reference Bodies

| Body | Country / Region | What they mainly decide |
|------|------------------|-------------------------|
| **NICE** | England | Cost-effectiveness (typically ~£20k–£30k per QALY) plus clinical value. Strong international reference. |
| **IQWiG / G-BA** | Germany | Added clinical benefit vs the appropriate comparator. G-BA decides; IQWiG usually assesses. Price negotiation follows the rating. |
| **HAS** | France | Clinical benefit and added clinical value; influences pricing and reimbursement. |
| **CADTH (CDA-AMC)** | Canada | Recommendations that influence provincial formulary decisions. Clinical + economic. |
| **PBAC** | Australia | Advises which medicines should be subsidised on the PBS. |
| **SMC** | Scotland | Similar role to NICE for NHS Scotland. |
| **EUnetHTA / EU JCA** | European network | Joint clinical assessments across EU member states; national pricing/reimbursement still local. |

## Egypt and Regional Context

| Authority | Role |
|-----------|------|
| **UPA** | Public procurement. Major influence on public hospital volumes and price benchmarks. |
| **UHIA** | Benefit packages and reimbursement under Universal Health Insurance. |
| **EDA** | Registration, pricing, quality, national formularies and drug policy. |
| **SFDA** (Saudi Arabia) | Increasingly structured HTA and pharmacoeconomic requirements. |
| **INEAS** (Tunisia) | One of the more established HTA bodies in the region. |

## How to Use This in the Field

- NICE is the most commonly cited international benchmark in sophisticated conversations.
- German IQWiG/G-BA is the main example of a **pure added-benefit** model (clinical first, price later).
- Egyptian decisions are **not** bound by NICE thresholds. Local priorities include budget impact, supply security, localization, clinical need, and UPA/UHIA processes.
- Formal HTA in Egypt is more developed for selected high-cost or innovative products than for typical hospital-line medicines.
- Knowing the language (QALY, ICER, added benefit, comparator, budget impact) builds credibility even when a full dossier is not required.
