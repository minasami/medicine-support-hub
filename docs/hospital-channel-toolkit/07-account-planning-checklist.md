# Account Planning Checklist – Hospital Channel

Use this checklist before important hospital visits, formulary discussions, or quarterly account reviews. Adapt depth to the size and strategic importance of the account.

---

## 1. Account Snapshot
- [ ] Account name and type (single hospital / group / specialty center)
- [ ] Key sites covered under this account
- [ ] Current formulary status of our relevant products
- [ ] Main competitors present and their relative position
- [ ] Approximate volume / potential for the portfolio in scope
- [ ] Any recent tenders, group contracts, or protocol changes

## 2. Decision Structure
- [ ] Central vs site-level decision making clarified
- [ ] Key stakeholders mapped:
  - Pharmacy (Chief Pharmacist / Procurement)
  - Medical / Clinical leads or department heads
  - P&T or formulary committee members (if known)
  - Group-level decision makers (if applicable)
- [ ] Primary clinical champion(s) identified
- [ ] Economic / procurement decision maker identified
- [ ] Relationship strength and history noted for each key contact

## 3. Clinical & Product Fit
- [ ] Current treatment protocols or guidelines used in the account
- [ ] Clear clinical differentiation points prepared (1–3 maximum)
- [ ] Relevant supporting data or summaries ready
- [ ] Any open clinical questions or objections from previous visits

## 4. Quality, Supply & Operations
- [ ] Supply performance history with this account reviewed
- [ ] Any past stock or quality issues and how they were resolved
- [ ] Documentation package ready (certificates, stability, etc. if typically requested)
- [ ] Track-and-trace / coding readiness confirmed if relevant

## 5. Economic Framing
- [ ] Current pricing / tender position understood
- [ ] Simple budget impact or total cost of therapy view prepared (if premium or competitive situation)
- [ ] Competitive price landscape known
- [ ] Any contract or volume commitment opportunities identified

## 6. Meeting Objectives
- [ ] Primary objective for this interaction defined (e.g. formulary discussion, adoption support, relationship building, issue resolution)
- [ ] Secondary objectives listed
- [ ] Clear “ask” prepared
- [ ] Desired next step defined in advance

## 7. Materials & Support
- [ ] Leave-behind materials selected and up to date
- [ ] Medical / training support available if needed
- [ ] Internal resources briefed (medical, supply chain, management) if escalation may be required

## 8. Post-Meeting Follow-up
- [ ] Immediate actions and owners agreed
- [ ] Follow-up date set
- [ ] CRM / account notes updated
- [ ] Any internal escalations or support requests logged

---

### Quick Pre-Call Version (5 minutes)
If time is limited, at minimum confirm:
1. Who am I meeting and what is their role in the decision?
2. What is the current status of our product(s)?
3. What is the single most important objective of this meeting?
4. What is the one thing I want them to remember or agree to?

---

*Tip: Review this checklist the evening before strategic meetings. Consistency in preparation compounds over time.*
