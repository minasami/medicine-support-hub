# Hospital Channel & Market Access Toolkit

**Purpose**  
Practical knowledge base and field tools for Key Account Managers, Sales, and Marketing teams working the hospital / institutional channel in Egypt.

This toolkit supports the Medicine Support Hub mission of making medicine information, access, and support easier for everyone — by equipping the professionals who influence formulary decisions, institutional access, and product availability in hospitals.

## Who This Is For
- Key Account Managers (Hospital / Institutional line)
- Sales representatives covering private hospital groups and key accounts
- Marketing and Medical teams supporting hospital channel strategy
- Anyone preparing for formulary discussions, P&T meetings, or account planning

## How to Use
1. **Before a hospital visit or key meeting** → Use the Account Planning Checklist + read Industry Trends and Formulary Drivers.
2. **When positioning value or access** → Use the Market Access overview + Pharmacoeconomic methods.
3. **For talking points** → Adapt the templates in `06-talking-points-templates.md` to the specific product and account.
4. **For deeper context** → Review NICE vs Egyptian HTA, HTA bodies, and IQWiG vs NICE.

## Guiding Principles
- Keep language practical and usable in the field.
- Prefer evidence and system understanding over pure product claims.
- Align with Egyptian realities (UPA, UHIA, private hospital groups, localization, quality requirements).
- Support long-term relationship building with stakeholders.
